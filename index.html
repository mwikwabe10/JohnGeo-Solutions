Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our services, data, deliverables and how we work with clients.

What services does JohnGeo Solutions provide?
We deliver remote sensing and land-cover change analysis, GIS mapping, boundary and rangeland analysis, energy and infrastructure site analysis, environmental forensics, and flood-risk and terrain modelling across East Africa.
Can your spatial work support NEMA environmental compliance?
Yes. We produce the maps, baseline analyses and spatial evidence that environmental assessments, audits and monitoring reports rely on. Formal NEMA submissions still follow the requirements of the registered experts and the project proponent, and we can work alongside them.
Which data and tools do you use?
We work with open satellite archives such as Landsat and Sentinel-2, elevation models, GNSS and field survey data, and your own datasets. High-resolution commercial imagery can be added where the project needs it.
What do I need to provide to get started?
Share your study area (a shapefile, KML or coordinates are ideal), your objectives, the time period of interest and your deadline. Any existing maps, reports or field data help us scope the work faster.
How long does a project take?
Timelines depend on the size of the area, the number of years or scenes analysed, the data available and the accuracy you need. Every proposal states a clear schedule and key milestones before work begins.
What will I receive at the end?
Typical deliverables are print-ready maps, GIS layers (GeoTIFF, Shapefile or GeoPackage), summary tables such as transition matrices, and a technical report that explains the method and findings.
How accurate are your results?
We check classified outputs against reference points and available field data, and report an accuracy assessment so you know how far the results can be relied on.
Do you work outside Kenya and East Africa?
Yes. We are based in Kenya and do much of our work across East Africa, but we serve clients globally. Remote sensing and GIS analysis can be delivered for almost any region. Send us your study area and we will confirm the scope and timeline.
How do I request a quote?
Use the inquiry form in the Contact section, or message us on WhatsApp. Describe your location, goals and timeline, and we will reply with a technical proposal.
