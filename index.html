SEC 01 — ABOUT

We bridge the gap between raw geospatial data and environmental action.

Based in Nairobi, JohnGeo Solutions Ltd works with developers, NGOs and public agencies across East Africa, combining remote sensing and GIS technology to support decisions that hold up to scrutiny.

Every deliverable — from a land-cover classification to a full impact assessment — is built to NEMA standards and structured so non-specialists can read it as easily as a technical review panel can.
